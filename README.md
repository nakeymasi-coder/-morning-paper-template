# 🗞️ Morning Paper — My Daily Newspaper

A customizable daily newspaper you can make your own: name your paper, pick your colors, paper textures, headline styles and edges, add your own sections, photos with captions, announcements and ad boxes — then print it or share it.

**Install it on your phone:** open the live page, tap Share (iPhone) or the ⋮ menu (Android), and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It installs as its own app with its own icon, and remembers your design on your device.
